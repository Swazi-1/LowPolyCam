# 🚀 LowPolyCam v2.0.0

A major update with **Pro Tools, live zoom, better battery life, and a completely refreshed interface.**

## ✨ What's New

* **Live Zoom** — Switch between **0.5×, 1×, 2×, and 5×** while recording.
* **Pro Tools** — Adjust exposure and white balance while filming.
* **Horizon Level** — Keep your shots straight with a live gyroscope level and haptic feedback.
* **Auto-Dim** — Automatically turns the screen black after 10 seconds while recording to save battery and reduce heat.
* **Manual Screen Dim** — Use the Moon button to turn the screen black anytime.
* **Custom Themes** — Choose **Lens Mint, Dial Lavender, Button Gold,** or **Record Red**.
* **Volume Shutter** — Use the Volume Up or Down buttons to start and stop recording.
* **In-App Preview** — Watch your latest recordings without leaving LowPolyCam.
* **3×3 Grid** — Enable gridlines for easier framing.
* **Slo-Mo** — Record at **120 or 240 fps**.
* **File Splitting** — Choose continuous recording or automatic 1-hour / 4-hour segments.

## 🎛️ Pro Tools

* **Exposure:** −2.0 to +2.0 EV
* **White Balance:** Auto, Sunny, Warm, Cool, Golden
* **Horizon Level:** Live gyroscope level with haptic feedback
* **Zoom:** 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 5×

## 🔋 Battery & Storage

* Auto-dim reduces screen power and heat during long recordings.
* Recording automatically stops below **300 MB** of free storage.
* HEVC provides smaller files while keeping good image quality.
* **720p Ultra Low:** about **123 MB/hour**.

## 🛡️ Reliability

* Recordings are written in small fragments to reduce data loss.
* Interrupted recordings are automatically recovered when LowPolyCam starts.
* Improved camera switching, OIS, file splitting, and frame handling.
* Unsupported camera settings are automatically disabled.

## 🎨 New Design

* New low-poly interface inspired by the LowPolyCam icon.
* Refined geometric controls.
* Frosted-glass Pro Tools drawer.
* Dynamic theme colors across the entire interface.
* Cleaner Settings with simpler descriptions.
* Smaller, auto-dismissing notifications.

## 🐛 Fixes & Improvements

* Fixed flashlight brightness and lock-screen state.
* Fixed camera switching and zoom flicker.
* Fixed OIS stopping during recording.
* Fixed long-recording file splitting.
* Fixed front-camera orientation.
* Improved virtual-lens white balance.
* Reduced background processing when Pro Tools are disabled.
